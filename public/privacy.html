Prompt.Up.Loudly

Privacy & safety

Prompt.Up.Loudly helps students aged 13 and older learn to write better AI prompts. We built it so that we don't need to know who you are.

For students: the short version

  • ✅ No sign-up, no account, no password.
  • ✅ We never ask for your name, age, school, email, phone or where you live.
  • ✅ If you type something personal, Vibo stops it before it goes anywhere and asks you to take it out.
  • ✅ No ads, no cookies, no tracking, no analytics.
  • ✅ Your badges are saved only in this browser. You can clear them any time in 🏅 My progress.
  • 🛡️ Golden rule: never share personal details with any AI tool, here or anywhere else.

For parents and schools: the details

What we collect

No personal information. There are no accounts and no forms, and we don't ask for any identifying details. We don't use cookies, advertising, analytics, fingerprinting or third-party scripts.

What goes to the AI

Only in ⚡ Power-Up, and only the app idea and prompt text the student types. That text is checked on the device and again on our server. Anything that looks like a name, school, email, phone number, ID number, address, birthday, social handle or link is blocked and never sent. The AI service we use is shown here when AI mode is on. We send it no student identifiers, account IDs or IP addresses.

🗺️ Quests and 🔍 Spot the gap run entirely in the browser, with no AI and nothing sent.

What we store

On our server: nothing about students. We don't save or log prompts. To stop overuse, the server counts Power-Ups per visitor per day using a scrambled one-way code that changes every day and stays in memory only. Our web host may keep standard security logs (such as IP addresses) for a short time.

On the student's device: badges and counts in the browser's local storage, never prompt text. "Clear my progress" deletes it.

Generated apps

Apps the AI builds run in a locked sandbox. They can't reach the internet, read this site, open pop-ups or collect data.

Content safety

Ideas must be school-appropriate. Unsafe topics are blocked before reaching the AI, and the AI is told to keep everything suitable for students aged 13+. If a student types something that suggests they may be at risk, Vibo gently points them to a trusted adult and helplines (988 in the US, Tele-MANAS 14416 in India).

Laws we designed for

Because we collect no personal information, there is no student data to share, sell or profile. We designed with these laws in mind: the US COPPA and FERPA, and India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, which treats everyone under 18 as a child and forbids tracking, behavioural monitoring and targeted ads for children. We do none of these. This page explains our design and is not legal advice.

Outside tools

"Open Gemini" takes students to Google's Gemini, which has its own terms and age rules. Students should follow their school's rules on which AI tools they may use.
